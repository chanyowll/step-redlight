How to play

  • 1
    Know your colour.

    Everyone is split into colour groups at random. Each colour hunts one colour (its prey) and is hunted by another (its predator). Your phone shows both.

  • 2
    Touch to eat.

    Green ring: you can eat them. Red ring: they can eat you. No ring: you just pass each other. Each eat moves you toward the next level.

  • 3
    Levels change the rules.

    Higher levels can eat lower levels of any colour, but your predator stays dangerous until you are 2 levels above them.

  • 4
    Big is slow.

    Each level makes you bigger and a lot slower (level 5 runs at under half the speed of level 1). From level 4 you no longer fit through the narrow alleys and park paths. From level 3 you get a DASH (10 second cooldown): drag the button the way you want to lunge.

  • 5
    Eaten? Back in 3 seconds.

    You drop one level and come back with a short shield. Grab pandesal for a little XP, and KAPE to eat your predator colour for 6 seconds.

  • 6
    Brownout!

    In the last 30 seconds the lights go out: everyone sees less, and every eat is worth double. The player with the most points wins; the crown is worth double too.
