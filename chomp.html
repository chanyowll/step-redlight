How to play

  • 1
    One taya.

    One player is the taya, wearing red with a pulsing red ring. The taya chases everyone else. Everyone else is a runner.

  • 2
    Touch to pass it on.

    When the taya touches a runner, that runner becomes the new taya. The new taya is the one who has to chase now.

  • 3
    5 seconds to run.

    The player who just stopped being taya is safe for 5 seconds. The new taya cannot tag them back, so they get a head start.

  • 4
    The taya is special.

    Only the taya can DASH (drag the button the way you want to lunge, 10 second cooldown). The taya also sees a wider part of the map and is a little faster.

  • 5
    Hide in the bushes.

    Step into a bush and nobody outside it can see you, and you cannot be seen. If you are the taya, runners in a bush vanish from your view. Touching still counts, so do not stay too long.

  • 6
    Run, run, run.

    Your score is the seconds you spend running minus the seconds you spend as taya. The most points wins. The team with the best average wins the team ranking.
